At Flora, we believe flowers are a language of their own — one that says what words sometimes can't. A single rose can say "I love you", a small bouquet can say "I'm sorry", and a handwritten card can turn an ordinary day into the best day of someone's year.

Our story

Flora started with a simple question: why should a beautiful gift be hard to send? We kept noticing that people wanted to make their loved ones happy, but struggled to find fresh flowers, elegant wrapping, and someone who would deliver exactly on time. So we decided to build the place we always wished existed.

From day one we made ourselves one promise: every bouquet that leaves our studio must be one we would proudly give ourselves. We hand-pick our flowers every morning from the best farms, arrange them with love and patience, and never hand over an order until we're 100% happy with it.

We don't just sell flowers… we deliver feelings

Behind every order there's a story: a mother welcoming her first baby, a friend celebrating a graduation, a husband remembering their tenth anniversary, or someone saying "I miss you" to a person far away. We know these moments don't come twice — so we treat every order as if it were our only one.

What makes Flora different?
